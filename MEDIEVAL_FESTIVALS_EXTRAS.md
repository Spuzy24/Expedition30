# 🐉 Extras — LARP, Fantasy, Viking, Roman, Celtic & Adjacent Scenes

Companion to [`MEDIEVAL_FESTIVALS_EUROPE.md`](./MEDIEVAL_FESTIVALS_EUROPE.md).

Everything here shares the *vibe* — costume, camps, swords, fire, another century — but isn't strictly a medieval market. If you liked Koprivnica, several of these will hit even harder, especially the LARP and Viking scenes.

**Compiled:** 24 August 2026 · dates are 2026 confirmed unless marked otherwise · 🔁 = fixed annual slot, safe to plan 2027 around

## 1. ⚔️ LARP — Live Action Roleplay

This is the deep end: you don't watch, you *play*. Multi-day, in-character, thousands of participants, purpose-built tent cities. Germany dominates.

| Event | Where | 2026 dates | Scale | Notes |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| **ConQuest of Mythodea** ⭐⭐⭐ | Gut Brokeloh, near Hanover 🇩🇪 | **5–9 Aug** (early arrival from 2 Aug; game starts Wed 5 Aug ~18:30) 🔁 | **~6,200 players + 2,000 NPCs** | **The largest LARP in the world.** Persistent fantasy setting, enormous faction camps, siege battles. If you do one LARP in your life, this is it |
| **DrachenFest** ⭐⭐ | Lake Edersee, Waldeck 🇩🇪 | **26 Jul – 2 Aug** 🔁 | thousands | ConQuest's great rival. Players join dragon-aligned factions; massive pitched battles and a spectacular tent city |
| **Epic Empires** | Hessen 🇩🇪 | summer 🗓️ | large | Long-running German fantasy LARP |
| **Mittelalter Welt Convent** | Schwarzenbach 🇩🇪 | Aug 🗓️ | medium | |
| **Bicolline — Grande Bataille** | near Shawinigan, Québec 🇨🇦 | summer 🗓️ | North America's largest | Purpose-built medieval village: taverns, guild halls, tournaments, feasts, faction warfare. Worth knowing about |
| **College of Wizardry** | Czocha Castle 🇵🇱 | multiple runs/year 🎟️ | ~150 | Wizard-school LARP in a real castle. Blockbuster "blockbuster larp" — books out fast |
| **Dziobak Larp Studios runs** | various 🇵🇱🇩🇰🇮🇹 | year-round 🎟️ | 50–200 | High-production narrative LARPs (Fairweather Manor, Convention of Thorns, etc.) |

**How to get into it:** LARPs need signup months ahead and a costume ("kit"). Start with a **one-day or weekend Croatian/Slovenian LARP** or a beginner-friendly German event before jumping to ConQuest.

**LARP calendars:** [larp-radar.com](https://larp-radar.com/) · [sevenswords.uk/larp-events-date-guide](https://sevenswords.uk/larp-events-date-guide/) · [larpshopkeeper.com/blogs/events](https://larpshopkeeper.com/blogs/events/best-larp-events-in-2026-worldwide) · [larp.info](https://larp.info/)

## 8. 🛡️ Combat sports adjacent (if you want to *do* it, not watch)

| Thing | What it is | Where to look |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **HMB / Buhurt** | Full-contact steel armoured team fighting. Croatia and Serbia both field teams; the Smederevo festival hosts bohurt battles | [Buhurt League](https://buhurt.com/) · IMCF · HMBIA |
| **Battle of the Nations** | The world championship of medieval combat | [botn.info](https://botn.info/) |
| **HEMA** | Historical European Martial Arts — sword fighting from period manuals. Clubs in Zagreb, Ljubljana, Vienna, Budapest | [hemaratings.com](https://hemaratings.com/clubs/) |
| **Historical archery** | Most Croatian knight associations run open archery competitions at their festivals | via [cuvari.hr](https://cuvari.hr/) |

> 💡 **The single best move if you loved Koprivnica:** contact one of the Croatian knight associations. [Red Čuvara grada Zagreba](https://cuvari.hr/kalendar-dogadanja/)'s 2026 calendar alone took them to Italy, Romania, Slovenia and Slovakia. That's how you get *inside* these events rather than watching from the rope line.
