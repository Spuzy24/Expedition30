## 0. Executive summary: what changed in 2026

These are the facts a 2025-era source would get wrong.

1. **Air China now flies from Zagreb.** The route is Beijing–Bucharest–Zagreb (CA913/914), 3× weekly (Mon/Wed/Fri) on an A330-200, since **4 Sep 2026**.
   - Schedule: ZAG 11:55 → OTP → PEK 08:05+1, about 14 h elapsed including the Bucharest stop.
   - Air China carries local traffic between Bucharest and Zagreb.
   - `[verified: Zagreb Airport airline list and timetable destination list (Beijing Capital, Bucharest) read 2026-10-04 — https://www.zagreb-airport.hr/en/passengers/flight-information/airlines/66 ; AeroRoutes 02JUL26 https://www.aeroroutes.com/eng/260702-casep26eu ; CroatiaWeek 19 Aug 2026 https://www.croatiaweek.com/beijing-zagreb-new-air-route-air-china-september-2026/]`
   - **Why it matters:** this is the first one-ticket Zagreb → Japan option on a Chinese carrier, the carrier type that historically gives the cheapest Europe–Japan fares. Onward from PEK, Air China still flies to **Haneda (~32/wk)** and **Kansai (2 daily)**; see section 2.2.
2. **Chinese carriers' Japan networks have been cut hard since Nov 2025.** The cause is the China–Japan diplomatic row after PM Takaichi's Taiwan remarks.
   - Air China cut about 56% of its Japan flights in NS26.
   - For NW26 Air China drops PEK–NRT/NGO/CTS/OKA and PVG–FUK/NGO/SDJ, among others. China Eastern cuts PKX–HND to 2/wk and drops PVG–HIJ and PVG–OKA.
   - Chinese carriers offered free refunds on Japan flights through 24 Oct 2026.
   - `[claimed: AeroRoutes https://www.aeroroutes.com/eng/260129-cans26jp , https://www.aeroroutes.com/eng/260803-cnnw26jp ; news summaries via search]`
   - **Effect:** fewer Chinese-carrier routes into secondary Japanese airports. Demand from China to Japan is also suppressed, which plausibly keeps Europe–China–Japan connecting fares low `[unverified hypothesis]`.
3. **A Middle East conflict (roughly Mar–Jun 2026) disrupted Gulf carriers and fuel supply.**
   - Qatar Airways suspended Zagreb and Belgrade for over three months and returned on 16/17 Jun 2026. ZAG, BUD and BEG stay at **4× weekly on an A320** through NW26.
   - Turkish Airlines cut ZAG to 14/wk for NW26, citing jet-fuel shortages.
   - T'way cited fuel-price spikes when it cut its Zagreb season.
   - `[claimed: ex-yu aviation 17 Jun 2026 https://www.exyuaviation.com/2026/06/qatar-airways-returns-to-zagreb-and.html ; AeroRoutes QR NW26 https://www.aeroroutes.com/eng/260717-qrnw26eu ; ex-yu aviation 22 May 2026 https://www.exyuaviation.com/2026/05/turkish-airlines-reduces-operations-for.html]`
4. **T'way Air is now "Trinity Airways" (TW) and its Zagreb route has shrunk.**
   - The rebrand took effect on 10 Sep 2026; the IATA code stays TW.
   - The 2026 ICN–ZAG season was cut to **27 Jun – 29 Aug, 2× weekly** (planned: 3×/wk to 24 Oct).
   - It is not operating now: Zagreb Airport's timetable shows no Seoul destination on 2026-10-04.
   - The 2027 season is unknown; the airline is in "emergency management".
   - `[verified: ZAG timetable destination list; claimed: ex-yu aviation 24 Apr 2026 https://www.exyuaviation.com/2026/04/tway-air-slashes-zagreb-operations-amid.html ; AeroRoutes https://www.aeroroutes.com/eng/260210-twns26zag ; rebrand via travelextra/airdatanews]`
5. **Emirates does not fly to Zagreb.** flydubai does (Emirates codeshare, DXB connection). Several SEO and aggregator pages still claim Emirates flies ZAG; they are **STALE**. `[verified: ZAG airline list has FlyDubai, no Emirates; TCN 7 Jun 2025 https://total-croatia-news.com/?p=186259]`
6. **Japan's departure tax tripled to ¥3,000 from 1 Jul 2026.** The new rate applies to tickets *purchased* from 1 Jul 2026; tickets bought by 30 Jun keep ¥1,000. Passengers in transit who leave within 24 h are exempt. `[verified: MLIT/JTA page https://www.mlit.go.jp/kankocho/seisaku_seido/kihonkeikaku/inbound_kaifuku/ryokakuzei.html ; purchase-date rule: trafficnews https://trafficnews.jp/post/667615]`
7. **Austrian's Vienna–Narita flight is seasonal.** It runs late March to mid-October and resumes ~29 Mar 2027 at 4×/wk, rising to 7×/wk from 3 May 2027. **ANA Haneda–Vienna (NH205/206)** runs year-round and is Vienna's only winter nonstop. `[claimed: AeroRoutes https://www.aeroroutes.com/eng/260330-osns26inc , https://www.aeroroutes.com/eng/260401-lhosns27inc ; ANA VIE resumption Aug 2024 via japanaviationhub]`
8. **Air China fares often do not show on Google Flights or ITA Matrix.** They do show on airchina.com's flexible search. Travel-Dealz said this about a MXP→TYO fare of **€551 RT** for December 2026. **The booking agent must search airchina.com directly.** `[claimed: Travel-Dealz 17 Jul 2026 https://travel-dealz.com/ticker/121404/]`

### 1.3 When a secondary airport can be cheaper

- **Korean Air and Asiana via ICN** reach about 16 Japanese airports, and Chinese carriers via PVG/PEK reach several. These carriers often price Europe → secondary Japanese airport the **same or nearly the same as Tokyo**.
- Fly4free deal posts show this pattern:
  - BUD → Okinawa, Tokyo or Osaka on China Eastern or Air China from 2,084 PLN (≈€490), Apr–Jun 2026.
  - BUD → Tokyo, Nagoya, Fukuoka, Sapporo or Osaka from 2,110 PLN (≈€496), Golden Week 2026.
  - VIE → Sapporo, Nagoya or Osaka on Air China from 2,362 PLN (≈€556) in 2024.
  - Secret Flying snippet: BUD → Hiroshima €456, Okinawa €460, Fukuoka €467, May–Jun 2026 `[claimed: SF via search]`.
  - `[verified: fly4free.pl posts read directly — https://www.fly4free.pl/sushi-w-tokio-czy-plaze-okinawy-od-2084-pln/ (5 Feb 2026)]`
- **Use a secondary airport when the trip starts or ends near it** (Kyushu: FUK; Hokkaido: CTS; Okinawa: OKA; Hiroshima: HIJ). This avoids an extra domestic ticket or a Shinkansen ride; Tokyo–Osaka by Shinkansen is roughly ¥14,000 `[unverified]`.
- **After the NW26 cuts, Air China's Japan network is essentially HND, KIX and NRT only** (via PVG, CKG and HGH). Secondary-airport fares now come mostly from China Eastern via PVG and from Korean carriers via ICN.
- **NRT vs HND:** Haneda saves about 45–60 min and ~¥1,000 in transfer cost. Narita has the LCCs, the Finnair/LOT/Austrian/Swiss/KLM nonstops and Saudia, Uzbekistan and Air Astana. A cheaper NRT fare still wins unless the gap is under about €15–20.

**Effect of the Russian airspace closure** `[unverified, general knowledge]`:
- EU carriers have been banned from Russian airspace since Feb 2022, and Japanese carriers avoid it. Their Europe–Japan flights route via Central Asia/China or over the pole.
- Today's flight times: VIE–NRT about 12 h 20 m and WAW–NRT about 12 h 35 m (schedule arithmetic, summer time), versus roughly 11 h before 2022. Finnair lost the most, since HEL–Tokyo used to be about 9.5 h.
- **Chinese carriers can still overfly Russia.** Their Europe–China legs are short and cheap to operate, which is a structural reason they undercut nonstop fares. Turkish, Gulf and Central Asian carriers are unaffected.

### 5.1 Open-jaw (e.g., into Tokyo, out of Osaka)

- On carriers that serve both HND/NRT and KIX (Air China, China Eastern, Turkish, Qatar, Emirates, Finnair, KLM/AF, Korean, Cathay, EVA), an open jaw is normally priced as half a round trip to each city, so it costs about the same as a plain round trip `[unverified pattern]`.
- **Evidence:** fly4free multi-city deals priced like round trips:
  - BUD → Osaka, Tokyo, Okinawa → BUD on China Eastern/Shanghai Airlines plus Peach for 2,100 PLN (≈€495), Oct 2024.
  - BUD → Osaka + Tokyo on China Southern plus an ANA domestic flight for 2,687 PLN, Aug 2025.

### 5.3 Departure tax (International Tourist Tax)

- **¥3,000 per departure** on tickets **bought on or after 1 Jul 2026**; it was ¥1,000 before.
- It is collected in the ticket price (Japanese tax code is usually OI).
- Exempt: under-2s, and transit passengers departing within 24 h of arrival.
- Changing a ticket bought before 1 Jul can trigger the new rate.
- `[verified: MLIT/JTA https://www.mlit.go.jp/kankocho/seisaku_seido/kihonkeikaku/inbound_kaifuku/ryokakuzei.html ; trafficnews https://trafficnews.jp/post/667615 ; TravelVoice 30 Dec 2025]`
- **Effect:** about +€11 per Japan departure compared with 2025 tickets. When comparing fares, check that all options include it.

### 5.4 Entry requirements for a Croatian passport

- **Visa-free for 90 days** (all EU states). `[claimed: Wikipedia "Visa policy of Japan", citing MOFA; MOFA page blocked automated fetch]`
- **JESTA** (Japan's ESTA-style pre-travel authorisation) is **not in force**. The target is **fiscal 2028** (Apr 2028 – Mar 2029), with the fee not yet set. The 31 Jul 2026 immigration plan made no immediate change. **Not needed for a 2026–27 trip.** `[claimed: goasia.cc 1 Aug 2026 https://goasia.cc/en/news/japan-immigration-plan-jesta-2028-travel-authorization ; visasnews 24 Apr 2025]`
- **Visit Japan Web** (vjw-lp.digital.go.jp): optional online pre-registration for the immigration and customs QR codes. It saves time at NRT, HND and KIX and is free `[unverified details; check the current site]`.
