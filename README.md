# Bologna & Florence, Eaten Well

A single-file field guide to eating well in Bologna and Florence without tourist traps, fine dining or a €40 plate.

Open `index.html` in any browser (it works offline). It holds about 130 places with verdicts, prices, hours, stories, sources and Google Maps links, a section on the dishes themselves, the rules of the table, and a filterable master table at the bottom.

Research and fact-checking were done on 20–21 September 2026 from Italian local press, Bologna and Florence food writers, Reddit threads up to September 2026, official menus and Google Maps data. Hours and prices in small trattorie change without notice: phone before crossing town.

Hosted with GitHub Pages at https://spuzy24.github.io/Expedition30/ (add it to your phone home screen).
